# Eclipse v2.0.2

- Native Windows recording with GPU scaling, window capture and HDR-to-SDR support.
- Hide the Windows capture border where permitted by the system.
- Faster clip preparation and early previews in the library and clip notification.
- Fixed microphone and desktop audio crackling; improved separate audio tracks and APO support.
- Improved clip playback, trimming controls, quick deletion and closing previews by clicking outside.
- Higher-quality Discord exports up to 20 MB, preserving 1080p recordings and frame rates.
- Fixed copy-button visibility and release-notes closing flicker.
- Expanded crosshair library with categories, favorites, custom images and individual settings.
- Cleaner overlays, more reliable media controls and improved add-on handling.
- Fixed startup behavior, organized capture sources and improved translations.
